## Notes on images that are used

- `atacama-yungay`: the Earth photo shows hyperarid terrain about 50 km northeast of Calama, not the Yungay study area itself (no openly licensed photo of Yungay was found). The caption says so.
- `m-jezero`, `m-namib`, `m-rocknest`: cropped from wide panoramas (cropping only; no upscaling or retouching).
- `m-clays` (Mawrth Vallis) is a HiRISE **enhanced-colour** product, as labelled in its caption.
- An earlier candidate for Meridiani evaporites ("Burns cliff.jpg") was rejected because its sky pixels had been filled in; it was replaced with the unmodified Pancam image of El Capitan (PIA05478).
